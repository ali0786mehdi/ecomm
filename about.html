Est. 2018 · Bangalore, India

Born from a Passion
for Craft

SOLE started in a small workshop in Koramangala, Bangalore, where our founder Arjun Mehta spent three years perfecting the ideal sneaker. Fed up with choosing between comfort and aesthetics, he set out to build a brand that refused to compromise.

Today, SOLE is India's most loved minimalist footwear brand — trusted by architects, designers, creatives and everyday people who believe what you wear tells the world who you are.

Every pair is designed in-house, tested on real terrain, and crafted with materials we'd proudly wear ourselves.

Our Journey

The SOLE Timeline

2018

The Beginning

Arjun Mehta starts SOLE from a 400 sq ft workshop in Koramangala. First batch: 50 pairs, sold out in 3 days.

2019

First Flagship

SOLE opens its first physical store in Indiranagar, Bangalore. 500+ customers on launch day.

2021

Going Digital

Launched sole.store — our online home. Reached 5,000 customers across 30 cities in 6 months.

2023

Sustainability Pledge

Switched to 100% recycled packaging and carbon-neutral shipping on all orders above ₹2999.

2026

15,000+ Soles

Over 15,000 customers in 50+ cities. New season dropping every Friday. The journey continues.
