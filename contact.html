Standard delivery takes 2–5 business days across India. Express delivery (1–2 days) is available for metro cities. You'll receive a tracking link via SMS and email once your order ships.

We offer 30-day returns on unworn shoes in original packaging. Exchange for size issues is free — just contact us within 7 days of delivery and we'll sort it out.

Our shoes run true to size for most styles. Check the size guide on each product page for measurements. When in doubt, size up — we also offer free size exchanges.
